<section class="about-content">
    <div class="container">
        <div class="mission">
            <p>The Toronto Climate Observatory (TCO) is an interdisciplinary initiative hosted at the University of Toronto. Our mission is to reimagine how communities around the Greater Toronto Area (GTA) understand and adapt to the impacts of climate change, and support place-based, plural, and just climate action.</p>
        </div>
        
        <h2>Values</h2>
        
        <div class="values-grid">
            <div class="value-item">
                <h3>EQUITY & JUSTICE</h3>
                <p>We seek equitable responses and decision-making to the climate crisis to ensure basic human needs and rights for all and more just outcomes for those who are most impacted by climate change</p>
            </div>
            
            <div class="value-item">
                <h3>PLACE-BASED</h3>
                <p>We are committed to action and research that are grounded in place and informed by local and Indigenous knowledges, place-based problem solving and adaptation, and cultural relevance</p>
            </div>
            
            <div class="value-item">
                <h3>ACTION-ORIENTED</h3>
                <p>Our goal is to help affected communities monitor and respond to the impacts of climate change, and we design our projects and evaluate our progress according to these metrics</p>
            </div>
            
            <div class="value-item">
                <h3>ACCOUNTABILITY</h3>
                <p>We are accountable to each other and all those who work with us. We work towards developing purposeful and considerate research practices, and critically evaluate the benefits as well as the potential harms of our activities</p>
            </div>
        </div>
