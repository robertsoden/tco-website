  Dr. Paul Kushner is a Professor and Chair of the Department of Physics at the University of Toronto and a member of the School of the Environment. His research in the Earth, Atmospheric and Planetary Physics group focuses on climate dynamics and modelling, including downscaling tools that connect global climate science to the needs of engineers and planners, and on characterizing sampling biases and uncertainty in applied climate information.
