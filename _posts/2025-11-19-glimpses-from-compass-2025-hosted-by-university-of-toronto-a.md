📸Glimpses from COMPASS 2025 hosted by University of Toronto, as captured by (and featuring some) TCO members!

🔎Plus, a bonus highlight: On July 23rd, members of the TCO, together with several other collaborators from across universities, organized a one-day, hybrid workshop on Place-Based Climate Data at COMPASS 2025. This interdisciplinary workshop interrogated how place - as physical, cultural, spiritual, and artistic - shapes the creation, circulation, and application of climate data in pursuit of equitable climate futures. Thanks to Taneea S Agrawaal, Sarah Cooney, Mohammad Rashidujjaman Rifat, Tanis Grandison, Xiao Fu, Han Qiao, Tajanae Harris, and Robert Soden for spearheading this critical exploration at COMPASS! ✨
