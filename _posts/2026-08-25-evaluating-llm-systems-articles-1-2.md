Articles 1 and 2 of our series on evaluating LLM-enabled systems for the environment are now on Medium. Article 1 covers everything that happens before you build an LLM-enabled system – ideation, goal setting, and clarifying what these systems even do. Article 2 explores the initial prototyping and quantitative evaluation that happens as a team starts to build. Thanks again to our collaborators at Development Seed and Earth Genome.

- Article 1: [Before the Build](https://medium.com/@toronto_23618/before-the-build-establishing-shared-language-and-exploring-team-objectives-0e014d176b8a)
- Article 2: [Prototyping and Performance Metrics: Building and Evaluation as Iterative Processes](https://medium.com/@toronto_23618/prototyping-and-performance-metrics-building-and-evaluation-as-iterative-processes-582b94dcc23a)
