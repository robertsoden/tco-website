<p>A great first day at the 2024 Beatrice and Arthur Minden Symposium on the Environment: Ontario Climate Risk Workshop.<br><br>The Symposium brought together experts from academia, government, the private sector, NGOs, and the public to share knowledge and co-create a research agenda on climate risk in Southern Ontario.<br><br>Today’s opening remarks and plenary featured:<br>- [](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAADr03mABDkZzthLXf-izoNzLJbr60No8Ano)[Steve Easterbrook](https://www.linkedin.com/in/steve-easterbrook-02830b237/), University of Toronto<br>- [](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAAD3bqcBWzmzdo5QtPhYQAV0JDf-BmCYLh8)[Jessica Strauss](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jessicastrauss/), Public Safety Canada<br>- [](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAA8EMd8BZhVKj65gM5NqrlgwFwGe7L4oZPE)[Lisa MacTavish](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lisakmactavish/), City of Toronto<br>- [](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAAVLCswBtje41tWJonn7frDUm5XD8mbEeX0)[Ingrid Waldron, Ph.D.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ingrid-waldron-ph-d-2908a725/), McMaster University<br>- [](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAACQnILABKIFY0Gj232BT9nuJp5f3kfZyCsw)[Kerry-Ann Charles](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kerry-ann-charles-35416914b/), Cambium Indigenous Professional Services<br><br>Followed by 4 Sessions on:<br>1.
