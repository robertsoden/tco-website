<p>The first-ever Toronto Climate Summer School (TCSS) has been featured on the University of Toronto's Arts & Science website.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Led by Professor Robert Soden and Dr. Rohini Patel, the TCSS brought together students from a range of fields to explore how climate change is affecting the city of Toronto. From tracking methane emissions in the Junction to studying how extreme weather impacts housing and migration, students dug into real issues happening right here in our city.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This hands-on, six-week course gave students the tools to apply their knowledge and skills to local climate challenges.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
