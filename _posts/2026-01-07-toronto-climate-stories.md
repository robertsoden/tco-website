In the run-up to local elections this year, researchers at the Toronto Climate Observatory and some of our partners will be developing data visualization and storytelling pieces that capture Toronto's unique relationship to climate change.

To help us do this, we want to hear your stories. Share yours at [climatestories.to](https://climatestories.to/).

This project is a collaboration with the Toronto Environmental Alliance, the School of the Environment, and the School of Cities at the University of Toronto.
