We're hosting the Toronto Climate Data Summit from February 18 to 20, 2026, at the University of Toronto (St. George campus), and we're looking for people interested in presenting their work on climate and data in Southern Ontario.

We want to widely highlight the important work being done on climate and data in Southern Ontario, and we define data broadly: we're interested in traditional data research, but also in people working on oral histories, citizen science, art, and more.

Applications closed on January 20, 2026. [Learn more about the summit](https://forms.gle/sC2grcPLyfH4ia4R6).
