<p>The Toronto Climate Observatory (TCO) is hiring a part-time (50%) Research Coordinator to support the growing activities of this campus-wide research initiative. With professors and graduate students from multiple departments and faculties at the University of Toronto, we have developed an ambitious research agenda, online platform, and outreach program to monitor and communicate the accelerating impacts of climate change locally and around the world. Drawing on approaches ranging from climate modeling to human-centered design, economics, oral history, citizen science, and art/science collaboration, the TCO develops place-based approaches to understanding the effects of climate change in ways that are contextualized and meaningful to policy-makers and the public. Key aspects of the initiative include our commitments to sound science, interdisciplinary collaboration, climate justice, and public engagement. </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>***Responsibilities***</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>As a Research Coordinator, the successful candidate will have the unique opportunity to contribute to the development of the agenda, day-to-day operations, and partnership strategy of the Toronto Climate Observatory (TCO). </p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Specifically, they will be asked to:</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul class="wp-block-list"><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Assist with drafting communication and background materials describing the initiative for various audiences;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Identify and engage potential partners at the University of Toronto and other organizations within Ontario and the Greater Toronto Area;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Support with tasks related to day-to-day organizational and financial management of research activities; </li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Conduct literature reviews of key research areas;</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li>Contribute to funding proposals aimed at growing the staff and impact of the TCO.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The position will begin on or before October 15, 2024 and is initially funded for 6 months.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
