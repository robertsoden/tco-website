The Bay Street Climate Report (October 2024) examines the financed emissions of eighteen Toronto-based banks, pension funds, and asset managers. It finds that these institutions financed over $1.43 trillion CAD in fossil fuel companies in 2022, contributing to at least 1.44 billion tonnes of CO2 emissions – nearly twice Canada's total emissions and roughly 100 times those of the City of Toronto.
